# AGORA, ABOGADOS — Visual Constitution v1.0

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Authority
The visual expression of AGORA must communicate authority and competence through composition, typography, whitespace, hierarchy, and restraint.

```text
Visual Constitution
        ↓
Design System
        ↓
Component & Page Specification
        ↓
Technical Architecture
        ↓
Implementation
```

---

## 2. Core Aesthetic Pillars

### Editorial Restraint
- Layouts must read like a curated legal journal or architectural monograph rather than a generic promotional site.
- High typographic contrast and generous, deliberate margins.
- Clean grid structures with purposeful asymmetry where appropriate.

### Authentic Locality
- Anchor the firm in **Ciudad Juárez, Chihuahua, México** with dignity and architectural perspective.
- Visual elements may include architectural perspectives of the city and the *Plaza de la Mexicanidad* (monumental X) treated editorially.
- Avoid folkloric or provincial stereotypes.

### Contemporary Authoritativeness
- Feel established (25 years of experience, 7 lawyers, 2 partners) without feeling antiquated.
- Communicate cross-border competence (Mexico–United States commercial and personal legal issues) without pretending to be a multinational conglomerate.

---

## 3. Explicit Visual Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)
- **NO** courthouse columns, marble stairs, or neoclassic pediments.
- **NO** gavels or wooden mallets.
- **NO** scales of justice iconography scattered decoratively.
- **NO** generic handshake stock photography or suited people smiling in artificial conference rooms.
- **NO** generic corporate navy blue templates.
- **NO** excessive dark backgrounds or "cyber/tech" dark-mode aesthetics.
- **NO** excessive rounded card containers (`rounded-3xl` / pill cards everywhere).
- **NO** aggressive, colorful gradients or noisy background textures.
- **NO** distracting animations, scroll-hijacking, or continuous floating elements.

---

## 4. Imagery Direction
- **Primary Visuals:** Architectural lines, crisp natural light, editorial portraits (when provided), subtle textures of Ciudad Juárez.
- **Image Treatment:** Natural color grading, restrained saturation, high sharpness, documentary/editorial framing.
- **Placeholder Rule:** Any missing client photography must use crisp geometric framing placeholders clearly labeled `PENDING CLIENT DATA`, never generic stock photography.
